About

We are a theologicall progressive church that believes God’s love is for everyone. We foster an environment in which every child, adult, visitor and member is free to explore, question, ponder and experiment within the full grace of God's love as shown in the life, death and resurrection of Jesus Christ.

Church Mission

Our mission is to be a caring community of God’s people who live by Christ’s teachings, reach out to others to share the love of God, and celebrate the good news of Jesus Christ through worship, prayer, thought, and action.

Church Values

Worshipful, Spirituality, Community, Invitational, Learning and Joy
